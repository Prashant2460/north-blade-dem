About North & Blade Barbers

North & Blade Barbers opened its doors in Roanoke, Texas with one goal: bring genuine, old-school barbering back to the neighborhood. We pair time-honored tools and techniques with a modern eye for style, so every client leaves looking sharp and feeling confident.

From first-time visitors to regulars who've had the same chair for years, we treat every cut with the same care and attention to detail.
